    How it calculates (Lean terms)
    • Backward explosion: boxes × pack config = pieces → items (slabs, cakes, minis) → trays, rounded up, plus the production loss → batter oz → lb → whole drums per batter type.
    • Hours per area = units × time per unit with that area's standard crew; parallel resources (mixers, ovens, lines) share the load. Days = hours ÷ net hours per day (shifts × (shift − breaks)). The freezer runs 24 h.
    • Bottleneck (TOC): the area with the most days of work. It sets the pace; everything else is subordinated to it.
    • Takt vs cycle: takt = available time in the window ÷ trays ordered; if the bottleneck's cycle per tray is longer, the window is too short.
    • Earliest ship date (capable-to-promise): bottleneck days + one tray's flow time + freezing (12 h ≈ 0.5 day) + QA release, counted in working days.
    • WIP cap: trolleys per flow = slowest-step rate × loop time ÷ trays per trolley, + 1 buffer (Little's law). Without the cap, filling pushes until the trolleys run out and they queue in the FIFO cooling lane; past the maximum dwell they are scrap (COPQ).
    • Pull vs push: pull gives mixing one signal per drum, so about two drums per running line are in rotation and batter stays fresh; push fills every drum ahead.
    • Heijunka: the brownie packaging line runs either IWP or clams; more, smaller campaigns level the mix at the cost of changeovers.
